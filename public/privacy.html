Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Vastra Care ("we", "us", "our") provides an on-demand ironing, dry cleaning, and laundry pickup service through this website and mobile app (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name and mobile number, verified by a one-time code (OTP) sent via SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Service details: pickup/delivery address, apartment or unit number, and any special instructions you add to an order.
  • Order history: items booked, service type, pricing, and status of each order you place.
  • Payment information: we do not store card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — payments are processed by Cashfree, our payment gateway partner. We retain only the payment status and a transaction reference for your order.
  • Wallet and referral data: in-app wallet balance and referral codes, where you choose to use these features.

Who processes this data

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseStores your account, order, and address data in our database.
CashfreeProcesses UPI, card, and net banking payments. We never see or store your full payment credentials.
Fast2SMSDelivers OTP and order-status messages to your phone.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify it's really you via OTP.
  • To schedule pickups/deliveries and keep you updated on order status.
  • To process payments and wallet top-ups.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties beyond the service providers listed above, except where required by law.

Data retention

We keep your account and order history for as long as your account is active, so you can view past orders and re-order easily. You can request deletion at any time — see below.

Your rights

  • Access: you can view your profile, addresses, and order history at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Correction: you can update your name and addresses directly in the app.
  • Deletion: use "Delete My Account Permanently" in Profile → Danger Zone, or email us at shanthi.jayaraman7@gmail.com and we will delete your account and associated data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain records for accounting or legal purposes.

Children's privacy

The Service is intended for users who can enter into a service agreement (typically 18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
